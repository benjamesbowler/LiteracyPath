LiteracyPath Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 June 2026

LiteracyPath is an early-literacy and phonics learning tool used by teachers with their pupils (roughly ages 4–7). We care deeply about the privacy of children and the schools and families who trust us. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

LiteracyPath is designed to be used through a school or teacher. Schools and teachers act on behalf of parents to authorise the limited collection of pupil information described below, consistent with the “school consent” approach under the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar rules. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children outside of a teacher-managed classroom context.

Who we are

LiteracyPath is operated by Benjamin Bowler. If you have any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at benjamesbowler@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Teacher account information: the email address and display name a teacher provides when they create an account, and the school name they enter.
  • Pupil information (entered by the teacher): a pupil’s first name (or chosen display name), their class, and a simple three-picture “symbol password” used so young children can log in without typing.
  • Learning activity: a pupil’s progress, scores, stars, books read, and assessment results, so teachers can track learning and the app can pick the right next activity.

We do not collect a pupil’s email address, home address, phone number, precise location, photographs, or any sensitive personal information. We do not ask children for personal details.

What we do not do

  • We do not show advertising of any kind.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or behavioural-tracking services.
  • We do not build marketing profiles of children.

How we use information

Information is used only to operate the learning service: to sign pupils in, save and show their progress to their teacher, choose appropriate activities, and let teachers print certificates and reports. Teacher contact details are used to manage the account and for essential service messages.

How information is stored and shared

Data is stored using trusted infrastructure providers who process it only to host the service on our behalf:

  • Supabase — secure database and login services.
  • Vercel — website hosting.

We share personal information only with these service providers, or where required by law. A pupil’s information is visible to their own teacher and school. Because the symbol password is a simple picture code intended for very young children, it is visible to the managing teacher by design and should not be treated as a strong secret.

Data retention and deletion

We keep pupil and teacher information for as long as the account is active and the school wishes to retain it. A school, teacher, or parent may ask us to review, correct, or delete a child’s information at any time by emailing benjamesbowler@gmail.com. We will respond promptly and delete the information unless we are legally required to keep it.

Parents’ choices

Parents can, through their child’s teacher or by contacting us directly, review the personal information we hold about their child, ask us to stop collecting or using it, and request its deletion. Because access is arranged through the school, we may verify requests with the relevant teacher or school.

Security

We use access controls and reputable hosting providers to protect information. No online service can be perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to safeguard the limited data we hold and to keep it confined to each school.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, notify teachers. Continued use of LiteracyPath after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions, access requests, or deletion requests: benjamesbowler@gmail.com.
