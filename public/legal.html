Operator and contact

Literacy Guide is currently operated by Benjamin Bowler, trading as Literacy Guide. Email benjamesbowler@gmail.com for privacy, legal, security or accessibility matters.

Commercial boundary. The current service is a free beta. It does not take payments or accept paid online orders. A paid school contract will not be offered until the contracting identity, geographic service address, price and tax information, provider agreements and UK international-transfer safeguards have been completed and supplied to the customer.

How the beta is reviewed

Human checking is continuous and works by exception: current content is treated as accepted unless a reviewer reports a defect. A reported defect is quarantined from the learner experience until corrected. This operating rule does not replace a school’s own safeguarding, accessibility, DPIA or procurement decisions.

Notices and changes

Material changes are dated on the relevant page. Teachers accept a versioned Terms of Use and acknowledge a versioned Privacy Notice when requesting an account. Invited parents or guardians do the same before a learner link is created. Schools should save the versions that apply to their use.
