Cookies and browser storage

Effective and last checked: 21 August 2026

No consent banner is shown because Literacy Guide currently uses no advertising, cross-site tracking, marketing or optional analytics storage. The application uses browser storage only for services the user asks for, account security, classroom continuity, accessibility settings and safe delivery/recovery.

What this notice covers

UK rules cover cookies and any similar technology that stores information on, or reads information from, a phone, tablet or computer. Literacy Guide mainly uses local storage and IndexedDB rather than traditional cookies, so this notice describes all of them.

Anonymous try-out

Before the try-out starts, the app replaces its normal browser storage with temporary in-memory storage and disables database/authentication calls. That memory disappears when the page closes. Ordinary hosting request logs still exist as explained in the Privacy Notice.

Your choices

You can clear this site’s cookies and storage in your browser. Doing that may sign you out, remove unsynchronised work and reset accessibility choices. School-managed devices may restrict this setting. Because no optional tracking is active, there is no separate “reject optional cookies” control at present.

If optional analytics, advertising or another consent-requiring technology is proposed later, it must remain off until this inventory is updated and a valid choice mechanism is in place. Contact benjamesbowler@gmail.com if you see storage or a network recipient not described here.
