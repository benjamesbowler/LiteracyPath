# River Gardens Premium Scenery

This project-authored premium River Gardens scenery kit was produced for Sound
Seekers on 17 July 2026 with OpenAI image generation, then manually curated into
fixed transparent game canvases.

The six south-facing props share the chapter cast's river-teal, sage,
terracotta, coral, cream, lavender, and dark plum-brown visual language:

- `waterwheel-weir.png` (`160x160`)
- `lily-ferry.png` (`112x112`)
- `sluice-gate.png` (`128x128`)
- `canal-map.png` (`128x128`)
- `garden-arch.png` (`144x144`)
- `willow-bank.png` (`144x144`)

The generated six-cell source was split by its declared grid, chroma-keyed,
inspected for isolated components and colour fringe, point-sampled, and aligned
to fixed transparent canvases. These files are project-authored and are not part
of the upstream Ninja Adventure CC0 pack.
