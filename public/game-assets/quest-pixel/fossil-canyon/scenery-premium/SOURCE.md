# Fossil Canyon Premium Scenery

This project-authored Fossil Canyon scenery kit was produced for Sound Seekers
on 17 July 2026 with OpenAI image generation, then manually curated into fixed
transparent game canvases.

The seven props share the named cast's red-rock, ochre canvas, teal
trail-cloth, bone cream, amber-gold, and warm dark-brown visual language:

- `rib-arch.png` (`176x176`)
- `dig-camp.png` (`160x160`)
- `rope-bridge.png` (`176x176`)
- `bone-signal.png` (`176x176`)
- `amber-outcrop.png` (`144x144`)
- `survey-station.png` (`160x160`)
- `dig-basin.png` (`256x192`)

The generated six-cell scenery source and dedicated top-down dig-basin source
were chroma-keyed, inspected for isolated components and colour fringe,
point-sampled, and aligned to fixed transparent canvases. These files are
project-authored and are not part of the upstream Ninja Adventure CC0 pack.
