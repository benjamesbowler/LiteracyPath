# Seedwake Premium Scenery

This project-authored premium Seedwake scenery kit was produced for Sound Seekers on
17 July 2026 with OpenAI image generation, then manually curated into fixed,
transparent game canvases.

The source direction locks the kit to Seedwake's top-down 16-bit visual language:
sage and moss greens, terracotta, warm cream, muted lavender, dark plum-brown
outlines, south-facing anchors, clustered pixel shading, and no baked shadows.

Final game assets:

- `round-tree.png` (`128x128`)
- `blossom-tree.png` (`128x128`)
- `flower-shrub.png` (`96x96`)
- `hollow-tree.png` (`160x160`)
- `trail-ruin.png` (`112x112`)
- `seed-lantern.png` (`80x80`)

The generated six-cell sheet was split by its declared grid, chroma-keyed,
inspected for isolated components and colour fringe, downsampled with point
filtering, and aligned to transparent fixed-size canvases. These files are
project-authored and are not part of the upstream Ninja Adventure CC0 pack.
