# Sound Seekers book-world maps

Generated for Literacy Guide with OpenAI image generation on 30 July 2026.
These are original Literacy Guide environments. The supplied game screenshots
were used only as quality, density and top-down-mechanics references; no
characters, level layouts, labels or assets were copied.

## Art direction

Create a polished, richly illustrated top-down children's adventure map in the
original Literacy Guide storybook style. Make the paths and junction clear
enough for direct four-way play, with layered foliage, landmarks, readable
clearings and several routes suitable for mini-games. Use no text, UI,
characters or logos. Meadow is a warm farm-and-pond world; Dino is a lush
prehistoric valley with fossils and lava; Moonwood is a moonlit enchanted
forest with an observatory, lanterns and stone circles.

Landscape images are the full hub compositions. Portrait images are
runtime-compatible playable trail variants. Production WebP files were encoded
at quality 86 from the generated PNG sources.
