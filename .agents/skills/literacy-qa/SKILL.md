# Literacy QA

Find the active bank, generator, renderer and scoring path before changing an
item. Read only the applicable standards:

- Scored questions: [Question Design Bible](../../../docs/content/QUESTION_DESIGN_BIBLE.md).
- Skills assessment: [authoring](../../../docs/skills-assessment-rebuild/AUTHORING_STANDARDS.md)
  and [mastery](../../../docs/skills-assessment-rebuild/MASTERY_SYSTEM.md).
- Reading: [Guided Reading index](../../../docs/guided-reading/INDEX.md) or
  [Story Quest Bible](../../../docs/content/STORY_AND_STORY_QUEST_BIBLE.md).
- Reports: [Reporting Bible](../../../docs/reporting/REPORTING_BIBLE.md) and its
  live policy definitions; do not infer reading mastery from game completion.
- Learning games: [Game Design Bible](../../../docs/design/GAME_DESIGN_BIBLE.md).

Review the literal prompt, every option and its actual text/image/audio
stimulus together. Establish the construct, taught content and learner band;
try to justify each distractor. Sound, letter and spelling are distinct units.
An initial-sound target must match pronunciation at the beginning of the word.
Do not make picture guessing substitute for decoding or incidental reading
load substitute for phonological awareness.

Verify the runtime output after enrichment/shuffling, feedback, retry, resume
and saved evidence. Check sibling items generated by the same faulty rule.
Keep existing thresholds in their single live policy definition.

Use the `question-contracts` profile in [task gates](../../../docs/verification/TASK_GATES.md),
plus the applicable assessment, reading or reporting checks named by the
standard. For changed media or interactions, load the corresponding skill.
Report concrete item IDs, observed failures and the tests that exercised the
correction; passing structure alone does not prove educational validity.
