### Meadow Pals

  > Muddy sits upon the mat, / with a map of this and that. / Up the hill to find the moon, / Muddy hums a happy tune.
- `cycle-02.mp3` — "Woolly in the Sun"
  > Woolly naps out in the sun, / on top the hay - what fun! / Bouncy hops up to the tent, / and that is where the day was spent.
- `cycle-03.mp3` — "Clucky's Nest"
  > Clucky builds a cosy nest, / Tiny needs a little rest. / Muddy dips a fishing net, / then has a nap - not finished yet!

### Dino Pals
- `cycle-04.mp3` — "Quick Little Zippy"
  > Zippy is as quick as a fox, / a fish swims by the rocks. / No dog lives in the Hollow, no - / just dino pals on the go.
- `cycle-05.mp3` — "Dozy's Lamp"
  > An otter plays on top the log, / by Cozy Cave in the fog. / When night is dark and damp, / Dozy lights the little lamp.
- `cycle-06.mp3` — "Zippy Runs"
  > Zippy loves to run and run, / in a red hat in the sun. / Chompy stomps to the cave door, / then Zippy runs some more.

### Moonwood Tales
- `cycle-07.mp3` — "Into the Oak"
  > "Come into the Oak," Pip said, / "it's warm, and time for bed." / The Moonwood friends played in the sun, / until the day was done.
- `cycle-08.mp3` — "Stone's Ball"
  > Stone rolls the big round ball, / it bounces off the wall. / No wolf is near - just watch! / Fern and Pip cheer and clap.
- `cycle-09.mp3` — "Wren the Queen"
  > Wren is the spell-book queen, / the cleverest you've seen. / But Stone jumps up in the mud, / and lands with a gentle thud.

### Meadow Pals

### Dino Pals

### Moonwood Tales
