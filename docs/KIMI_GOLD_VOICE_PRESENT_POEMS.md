## Poems to record (27) — folder: `public/audio/learn-games/poems/v2/`

### Meadow Farm (cycles 1-9)
- `cycle-01.mp3` — "Muddy's Map"
  > Muddy sits upon the mat, / with a map of this and that. / Up the hill to find the moon, / Muddy hums a happy tune.
- `cycle-02.mp3` — "Woolly in the Sun"
  > Woolly naps out in the sun, / on top the hay - what fun! / Bouncy hops up to the tent, / and that is where the day was spent.
- `cycle-03.mp3` — "Clucky's Nest"
  > Clucky builds a cosy nest, / Tiny needs a little rest. / Muddy dips a fishing net, / then has a nap - not finished yet!
- `cycle-04.mp3` — "By the Farm Gate"
  > A fox trots past the old farm gate, / the fish in Duck Pond swim so straight. / The dog naps by the big red barn, / while Muddy digs across the farm.
- `cycle-05.mp3` — "The Otter's Visit"
  > An otter plays down by the pond, / and Splashy quacks - she's very fond. / On top the hay the hens all camp, / at night old Clucky lights the lamp.
- `cycle-06.mp3` — "Speedy Runs"
  > Speedy loves to run and run, / in a red hat in the sun. / Bouncy hops to the farm gate, / then they both run home - it's late!
- `cycle-07.mp3` — "Into the Barn"
  > "Come into the barn," Muddy said, / "it's warm, and time for bed." / The Meadow Pals played in the sun, / until the day was done.
- `cycle-08.mp3` — "Woolly's Ball"
  > Woolly rolls the big round ball, / it bounces off the garden wall. / No wolf is near - just watch the sheep, / they play until they fall asleep.
- `cycle-09.mp3` — "Clucky the Queen"
  > Clucky is the farmyard queen, / the proudest hen you've ever seen. / But Muddy jumps up in the mud, / and lands with a gentle thud.

### Dinosaur Valley (cycles 10-18)
- `cycle-10.mp3` — "Chompy's Cap"
  > Chompy found a comfy cap, / then Dozy took a valley nap. / A cat naps near a mountain goat, / beside the stream and little boat.
- `cycle-11.mp3` — "Count With Zippy"
  > Zippy counts to six - what fun! / A pig naps in the sun. / A fox runs past the ferny den, / then Zippy zooms again.
- `cycle-12.mp3` — "Off to the Vet"
  > Sunny finds a speckled egg, / poor Wiggly bumped his leg. / They drove the van to see the vet, / the kindest one they've met.
- `cycle-13.mp3` — "Zippy the Jet"
  > Sunny flies a bright red kite, / Zippy zooms - a dino jet! / Honky pours from a big jug, / and Dozy gives a sleepy hug.
- `cycle-14.mp3` — "This Is Sunny Hollow"
  > This is Sunny Hollow, see, / with dino pals like you and me. / Bring your friends to play all day, / in this warm and sunny way.
- `cycle-15.mp3` — "Chompy's Chip"
  > Chompy nibbles one big chip, / Honky dreams about a ship. / No sheep live in the Hollow here, / just dino pals to cheer!
- `cycle-16.mp3` — "One and All"
  > The dino pals all share the ball, / it bounces by the waterfall. / "Watch the little ant!" honks Honky's call, / "come play, come play - one and all!"
- `cycle-17.mp3` — "Little Cheeky"
  > Little Cheeky and friends all like / the leafy path, the valley hike. / "No igloo here!" laughs Zippy with glee, / "just warm volcano lights for me."
- `cycle-18.mp3` — "By Fossil Creek"
  > An otter swims where tall ferns grow more, / along Fossil Creek's green shore. / No ox lives in the Hollow, true, / just dino friends for me and you.

### Moonwood Forest (cycles 19-27)
- `cycle-19.mp3` — "Up the Great Oak"
  > Pip climbs up the Great Oak tall, / will put his cap beside the wall. / He splashes in the puddle mud, / and lands with a happy thud.
- `cycle-20.mp3` — "The Glowing Egg"
  > Wren has found a glowing egg, / "Get it safe!" said Pip, "I beg." / Luna nodded, wise and very, / "Carry it gently - and be merry."
- `cycle-21.mp3` — "Who Sings?"
  > Who sings soft in the moonlit glen? / What glows beyond the fen? / When Luna counts the stars to ten, / the Moonwood friends all play again.
- `cycle-22.mp3` — "Pink Dawn"
  > A pink dawn lights the Moonwood trees, / and mushrooms bob in the soft breeze. / Glimmer gives a glowing wink, / as berries float and never sink.
- `cycle-23.mp3` — "The Forest King"
  > The Great Oak is the forest king, / he loves to hear the Moonwood sing. / The friends all join the evening song, / and hum and gently sway along.
- `cycle-24.mp3` — "Off They Go"
  > The silver bell rings off - ding, ding! / Which Moonwood friend is first to spring? / Fern flutters by the Crystal Stream, / with Spark's bright farewell gleam.
- `cycle-25.mp3` — "Play Again"
  > "Let's play again!" the friends all say, / a brand new Moonwood day. / Pip and Luna read and play, / "hip hip hooray!" they say.
- `cycle-26.mp3` — "Spark Tries"
  > "Why does Glimmer's fire fly high?" / "My spark!" laughs Spark, "I'll try!" / Fern flutters way up high, / and waves the clouds goodbye.
- `cycle-27.mp3` — "First Friends"
  > Pip's best friend is here at last, / Fern flies in - she's first, so fast! / They share a berry, half each, / in the Whispering Meadow's reach.
