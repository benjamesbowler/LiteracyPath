# Small Steps: cumulative decodable readers

Small Steps is an original 20-book connected-reading sequence for children who
have been taught the declared code and exception words. Books begin after Cycle
10 and progress through Cycle 24 of `src/data/elSkillsBlockCycles.js`.
The individual scope is a teaching prerequisite, not evidence of learner mastery.
App text bands and the A/B/C language profile remain separate judgments.

The names are ordinary words in the reading scope, not automatic exceptions.
Secondary animals or props require a book-specific locked reference before art.
Do not reuse a name from another story world as an identity shortcut.

Use warm, clearly drawn gouache picture-book art with restrained paper texture,
confident brown contours, simple color shapes and expressive silhouettes. Keep
faces, clothing, scale and anatomy tied to the master. Scenes have concrete
settings and a clear focal action. No glossy 3D finish, decorative visual noise,
generated lettering, pseudo-labels or text embedded in the art. The reader lays
out the actual print separately. Page art is landscape and safe to display in
full; important objects remain inside the image edge.

## Reading and manuscript contract

Each book records its exact cumulative correspondences, explicitly taught
exception words, every unique title/story word's reviewed pronunciation and
grapheme alignment, and the earliest completed cycle that permits it.
Release requires zero undeclared words. Proper names, sound effects, inflections
and the title receive the same audit as the narrative.

Start with VC/CVC words and only the structures explicitly introduced by the
cycle sequence. Knowing letters alone does not authorize arbitrary consonant
clusters, suffixes, split digraphs, vowel teams, silent letters, or multisyllabic
words. `qu`, `x`, `nk`, `ng`, `all`, and doubled final consonants retain their
actual taught sound values. Do not certify words through substring matching or
the existing broad phonics analyzer's decodable percentage.

Use a small actual exception list per book, with each word's introducing cycle
and exceptional spelling explained. A taught whole word does not introduce its
spelling pattern generally. Do not silently whitelist the whole sight-word bank.

Write natural English with articles, clear pronouns and a complete micro-arc.
Change the premise if restricted code makes a draft awkward. Each story has a
concrete want, obstacle, meaningful attempt, observed turn and visible outcome.
Humor comes from action and a prepared payoff. Care, patience and repair arise
through events rather than an appended moral. Nonfiction may instead use an
accurate question, ordered comparison or process, and clear synthesis.

The initial sequence uses the Level B language envelope: 6–12 pages, no more
than 14 words or two lines per page. Aim for 6–8 purposeful pages and usually
10 or fewer words. Two short sentences are permitted; avoid artificial sentence
fragments. This label does not establish phonics eligibility or RAZ equivalence.

## Production and verification

Freeze text after independent editorial and scope review. Record a page state
ledger and exact setting, prop and character invariants before illustration.
Generate a distinct illustration for every reading page and a suitable cover.
Compare each image with the master and adjacent pages; inspect ambiguous details
at native size. Reject altered identities, extra limbs, changing object counts,
unexplained setting changes, and pictures that reveal an event before it happens.

Generate exact-text page narration and resolve every story word's audio. Keep
whole-word help when phoneme segmentation lacks a verified pronunciation; never
substitute letter names for sounds. File integrity, text matching and automated
speech recognition are recorded separately from human listening.

The teacher can see the completed-cycle prerequisite and actual exception words.
Children retain narration and supported reading. Do not infer independent
readiness from an app letter, age, a completed book or an unsupported default.
Recommendations must use the authored scope before calling a Small Steps book
independent practice. Exercise the finished books in the real reader, including
page navigation, text layout, narration and word help on narrow/mobile layouts.

Follow the current continuous QA policy. A known defect requires repair; missing
human-review metadata alone does not create a new approval queue. No automated
check establishes classroom outcomes or superiority over another publisher.
