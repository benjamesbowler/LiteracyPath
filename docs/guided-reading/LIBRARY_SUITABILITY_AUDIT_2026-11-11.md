# Guided Reading library audit, 11 November 2026

The additional review covered all **447 active books**, **3,941 ordered reading
pages**, their titles, picture descriptions, teaching support, and **4,388 cover
and page image slots**. The images were screened in contact sheets, with original
files opened for flagged details. The 27 Cycle Shorts and their questions and
artwork were reviewed separately. This was an AI editorial and visual review;
it does not establish human listening or classroom acceptance.

No explicit LGBTQI, religious or real political themes were identified in the
active material. Natural rainbows, pretend roles, ordinary friendships, geography
and clearly framed fantasy were assessed in context. The earlier
[five World Discovery topic replacements](WORLD_DISCOVERY_TOPIC_REPLACEMENTS_2026-11-10.md)
are the only whole-topic replacements recommended by these two reviews.

The audit recorded **33 local candidates across 31 books**. Of those, 17
description, brief and discussion findings were corrected, nine illustration
findings were repaired, six books received local wording corrections, and one
suspected bridge pose was retained after native review. The 15 replacement
illustrations and six cover renditions passed reader and library checks. The exact [metadata changes](../../source-art/guided-reading-remediation/2026-11-11/metadata-alignment.json),
[image repairs](../../source-art/guided-reading-remediation/2026-11-11/image-repairs.json)
and [Bag of Wind sequence](../../source-art/world-discovery/remediation/2026-11-11/d04-complete-book-review.json)
record the repaired source and reviewed bytes.

Six books received the following wording changes.

| Book | Clarification |
| --- | --- |
| *Aiden and Betty: New Teeth* | Say that Mom left the coin, so the gift has an explicit cause. |
| *What is Flappy?* | Use *Flappy Finds a Way*, which describes the climbing and gliding story. |
| *Stone Crosses the Bridge* | Name the bank where Pip and Stone started on the return crossing. |
| *Stone and Dewdrop and the Stuck Fish* | Have the fish swim in a circle nearby while the characters sit on the bank. |
| *Spark's Ten-Count Hook* | Explain that glue binds the root fibers as it dries. |
| *The Lunchbox Mix-Up* | Show that the boxes stayed with their owners and the contents were swapped. |

Three Cycle Short edits make the speaker clear in *My Red Hat*,
keep one tense in *The Tin in the Mud*, and clarify who rings the bell for lunch
in *The Picnic Bell*, previously *Which Bell?*. The books and shorts have matching
Leda narration. The [book narration receipts](../../source-art/guided-reading-remediation/2026-11-11/narration-review.json)
record the exact text, file hashes and audio checks.
