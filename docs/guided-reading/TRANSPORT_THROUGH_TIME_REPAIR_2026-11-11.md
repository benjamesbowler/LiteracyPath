# Transport Through Time: manuscript and image delivery repair

The owner reported that the reader showed the previous route-planning pictures
beside the replacement text, and that the replacement gave too much space to
trains and carts while omitting future vehicles.

The revised `world-discovery-e-21` manuscript keeps its title, stable ID, ten
pages and independently reviewed public level E. Its 202 words follow changes
in power and carrying purpose across carts, rowing and sailing boats, steam
trains, cars, buses, trucks, ships, aircraft, electric transport and cargo bikes.
Two pages explain supervised camera/sensor vehicle tests and experimental
electric aircraft, with conditional wording about lighter batteries. The ending
compares older, present and still-tested examples. It does not promise a future
launch date or describe prototypes as ordinary passenger services.

Every current reference, cover and page image has a separate exact-byte review.
Two suitable earlier illustrations are reused with their original generation
receipts preserved in `transport-art-reuse.json`; the remaining artwork was
generated for this manuscript. The fresh editorial review, card review, exact
Leda narration evidence and supported maintenance registration are under
`source-art/world-discovery/remediation/2026-11-11/`. Earlier dated evidence stays
historical. Automated recognition and signal checks do not establish human
listening or classroom acceptance.

The image mismatch was independently reproduced with the installed offline
worker: its media cache ignored query strings and returned an earlier picture
despite the new version query. Replacement pictures now use byte-identical,
content-addressed full-resolution delivery files. This changes the pathname
even for an already-active old worker; the corrected worker also respects image
revisions. Canonical catalogue and QA image identities stay unchanged. The
delivery producer checks 51 whole-topic replacement images and 15 accepted
related image repairs, and the visual inventory covers the actual delivery
paths as well as canonical sources. No learner records or browser caches are
cleared by the repair.

Registration is a local integrity step. Reader, device, PDF, build, required PR
checks, automatic deployment and live old-cache verification remain separate
release evidence in `.artifacts/transport-repair/`.
