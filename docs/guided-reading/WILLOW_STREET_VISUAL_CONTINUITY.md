## Illustrated-world lock

The first fourteen titles use a smooth, hand-painted 2D children's-book finish with clean silhouettes, gentle paper-like colour variation but no fake canvas grain, controlled depth, and believable hands and objects. Keep a warm neighbourhood palette: brick `#B7664D`, leaf `#5C8A58`, sky `#A9D6E5`, cream `#F4E7CC`, teal `#277C7A`, mustard `#D6A32B`, coral `#D96B62`, and cobalt `#3768A6`. Skin is rendered naturally without grey cast or over-saturation. Art contains no readable words, brand marks, watermarks, pseudo-lettering, or baked-in page text.

Children are six to seven years old, approximately five-and-a-half heads tall, with child-sized hands, feet, shoulders, and facial proportions. Relative height is fixed: Leo is tallest; Samir is one palm shorter; Maya and Zoe are equal and one palm shorter than Samir. Adults are visibly adult in height and proportion. Never alter skin tone, hair texture, body build, or wardrobe to create page-to-page novelty.

## Prop and action continuity

Every brief names the story-state objects that must persist. Artists must carry forward dents, wetness, food quantities, opened or closed containers, and the exact side of mechanical defects. The lunch items return to their owners; the lost book stays green; the windy picnic has exactly three napkins and four boxes; puddle cones remain two; the cart defect stays front-left; the gate stone moves from path to border; the kite remains blue with one ribbon tail; the ramp car remains red.

Adult supervision must be visible, not implied outside the frame, for tawa, boiling water, knives, scissors, glass transfer, oil, and machinery. Hands stay away from hinges, axles, rollers, engines, presses, and hot surfaces. No child climbs the ramp, enters a road, approaches wires, or operates industrial equipment.

In the lunchbox sequence, Maya keeps the yellow box and Samir keeps the green box. The blue cup begins inside yellow and the green apple inside green; page 4 exchanges the contents. The silver spoon stays beside Samir's orange napkin until returned. In the ramp sequence, retain one rigid board and one red hatchback. Blue over green books support only the high end; add one yellow book underneath for the second roll. The low end stays taped to the same hardwood landing surface. Blue marks the first stop and yellow the second, using the same point on the car for both. The illustration shows one ordering of stops, not a promise that every second roll travels farther.

## Stable print and composition

Reserve the lower 22 percent of every cover/page for the same quiet cream application-rendered text panel. Keep faces, hands, the causal object, and evidence out of that zone. Use one dominant action and at most two secondary evidence clusters per illustration. Covers show the premise, never the resolution. Page images cannot depend on visible labels or decorative pseudo-text.

## Photorealistic lock

The last six books use newly created editorial photorealism, not stock imitation and not the style of any named photographer, publisher, or living artist. Use plausible 35–70 mm documentary perspective, natural or soft industrial light, neutral white balance, authentic surface detail, and moderate depth of field that keeps the factual evidence readable. No HDR halos, miniature tilt-shift, impossible cutaways, cloned people, malformed hands, fused tools, floating parts, brand marks, safety violations, graphic emergencies, or misleading before/after continuity.

- Wheat remains botanically correct from heads to kernels; guarded machinery and adult-only baking are required.
- Rainwater flow follows gravity. Use separate landscapes or honest connected views rather than arrows baked into art.
- Firefighters wear complete plausible gear. Dispatch displays contain no personal data; training scenes remain non-graphic.
- Paper machines have guards, continuous material flow, correctly sized rollers, and believable wet pulp.
- The snail keeps one shell pattern across all pages, four tentacles when the head is fully extended, eyes only at upper tips, one muscular foot, and natural mucus.
- Book production keeps page imposition, folding, spine, and binding mechanically plausible; all manuscript and screen text is illegible.
