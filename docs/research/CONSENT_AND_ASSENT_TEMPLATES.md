# Consent and assent templates

These are research-operational drafts, not legal advice or universal consent
forms. The study owner must obtain local legal/ethics, data-protection, school
and safeguarding review before use. Replace bracketed fields with approved
study-specific wording; never ask a child or family to sign an incomplete form.

## Template B: parent or guardian information and consent

**Why are we asking?**  
Your child’s school is considering a small study of LiteracyPath reading
activities. We want to learn whether the activities are clear, usable and
promising enough for further study. This is not a diagnostic test of your child.

**What will happen?**  
If you agree and your child also agrees, your child will complete short reading
and app-use sessions described in the attached schedule. A trained adult will
observe task completion, responses, timing and use of help. The child may take a
break or stop at any time.

**What data will be collected?**  
The research dataset uses a participant code, age/grade band, declared subgroup
flags, session/version details, scored response status, response time, support
stage and structured usability observations. It does not accept names, contact
details, addresses or dates of birth. [Describe separately approved recording,
if any; omit recording when not approved.]

**Choice and withdrawal**  
Taking part is voluntary. Saying no or stopping will not affect school,
teaching, services or access to LiteracyPath. You may withdraw until [approved
cut-off and reason]. Explain whether already aggregated anonymous results can be
removed after that point.

**Risks and benefits**  
Possible burdens include tiredness, frustration or discomfort being observed.
The facilitator will offer breaks and stop when requested or needed. There is no
guaranteed direct benefit. The study may help improve the activities.

**Privacy and retention**  
The identity key is stored separately by [approved controller], accessible to
[roles], until [date]. De-identified study data is stored in [region/system],
accessible to [roles/processors], until [date], then deleted under [procedure].
Results use grouped data and suppress small cells.

**Questions, complaints and data rights**  
Contact [study owner], [school safeguarding contact], and [data-protection or
ethics contact] using the approved details on the issued form.

Consent statements:

- I have read the complete approved information and had questions answered.
- I understand participation is voluntary and my child must also assent.
- I understand what data is collected, who can access it and when it is deleted.
- I understand withdrawal, complaint and data-rights procedures.
- I consent / do not consent to my child taking part.
- Separate optional recording consent: I consent / do not consent. A refusal
  does not prevent non-recorded participation.

**Guardian name/signature/date and child participant code:** [complete on the
controlled consent record; do not copy the name into research exports]

## Template C: child information and assent

Use large readable text and read aloud:

**We are trying reading activities.**

- We want to find what is easy and what is confusing.
- This is not a test of whether you are a good reader.
- You can ask a question, take a break, or stop.
- You can say no now or later.
- Nothing bad happens if you say no.
- We will use a code instead of your name in the research file.

Ask:

1. What can you do if you want a break or want to stop?
2. Do you want to try the activity?

Record: assented / declined / unclear. An unclear response is not assent.

**Child mark or verbal assent record, facilitator code, date/time, participant
code:** [complete on the controlled assent record]

At every later session ask: “Do you still want to do the activity today?” Record
the answer.

## Template D: teacher participant consent

The teacher workflow study observes how teachers navigate, interpret evidence,
recover from errors and use reports. It evaluates the product, not job
performance. School managers will receive only the approved grouped or redacted
output unless a separate lawful agreement states otherwise.

The teacher is informed of:

- tasks, duration and any screen/audio recording;
- data fields and redaction process;
- who can see raw observations;
- voluntary participation and withdrawal;
- retention/deletion;
- reporting and quotation choices;
- complaint and data-rights contacts.

Consent statements:

- I understand the product, not my professional performance, is under study.
- I may pause, decline a task or withdraw under the approved procedure.
- I consent / do not consent to observation.
- I separately consent / do not consent to attributed quotation.
- I separately consent / do not consent to recording.

outside the research export]
