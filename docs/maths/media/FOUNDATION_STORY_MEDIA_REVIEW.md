# Foundation number-story media review

Status: accepted for the Foundation release on 2026-08-13.

The two released stories have sixteen project-local 1200 × 900 WebP page illustrations. The final set is visible in `foundation-story-contact-sheet.webp`.

## Visual and mathematical checks

### Five Buns for the Picnic

- Pages 1–2: five buns are fully visible.
- Pages 3–5: the five buns are shown as two and three.
- Page 6: three are on the cloth, one remains in the basket and one is being moved.
- Page 7: the five buns are shown as three and two.
- Page 8: five buns are visible for five meadow friends.
- Cuddly remains a hedgehog and Splashy remains a yellow duck.

### Ten Lights at the Barn

- Page 1: ten empty hooks are shown in two rows of five.
- Pages 2–3: five top-row lights and five empty lower hooks are visible.
- Pages 4–5: seven lights and three empty hooks are visible.
- Page 6: the last three lanterns are grouped beside the safe ground-level pole action; the exact ten-frame remains in the interactive model.
- Pages 7–8: ten lights are shown as five and five.
- Muddy remains a pink pig and Splashy remains a yellow duck.

## Rejection rule

Drafts with incorrect counts, hidden countable objects, character drift, text, borders or watermarks were not copied into the project. The final page model remains visible over every illustration so the instructional representation is deterministic rather than inferred from generated art alone.
