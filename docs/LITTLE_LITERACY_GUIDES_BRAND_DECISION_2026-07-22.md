# Little Literacy Guides - Child-Area Brand Decision

**Decision date:** 2026-07-22  
**Status:** Approved child-area identity

## Brand architecture

- **Company and main site:** literacy.guide
- **Child area:** Little Literacy Guides
- **Primary lockup:** **Little Literacy Guides by literacy.guide**
- **Existing worlds:** Meadow Pals, Dino Pals, and Moonwood Tales
- **Mascot trio:** the orange rabbit, green dinosaur, and purple owl

The child area covers phonics, reading, stories, games, writing, assessment
practice, and rewards. The story-world names stay unchanged. Technical names
such as `pal-*`, `/images/pals/`, and `data-pal-world` also stay stable.

## Why this name

Little Literacy Guides is descriptive, friendly, and immediately clear to
families and teachers. It fits the existing character-led experience and keeps
the connection to literacy.guide visible without inventing a separate company.

Use the complete name in navigation, entry points, app-store copy, channel
profiles, and formal marketing. Do not shorten the public brand to “Little
Guides”, because that phrase is broad and already used in unrelated fields.

## Naming screen

The exact phrase produced no material conflicting literacy product in the
preliminary web, app, publishing, social, domain, and indexed-trademark screen
completed on 2026-07-22. The words are descriptive, so the name is a qualified
practical pass rather than a highly exclusive coined mark.

Keep the full endorsed form, **Little Literacy Guides by literacy.guide**, on
formal material. Before a paid public launch, commission a full professional
similarity search in the intended countries and relevant goods and services.
This preliminary screen is not legal advice or a trademark registration.

## Logo system

- **Primary transparent lockup:**
  `public/images/pals/little-literacy-guides-logo.webp`
- **Compact mascot mark:**
  `public/images/pals/little-literacy-guides-mark.webp`
- **Accessible lockup name:** “Little Literacy Guides by literacy.guide”
- **Minimum suggested lockup width:** 280 px
- **Preferred backgrounds:** open sky, cream, white, or deep forest green

The primary lockup uses the established three mascots, forest green wordmark,
tangerine accent, cream sign, and gold guide-star/open-book motif. The compact
mark is for favicons, profile pictures, tight headers, and spaces where the full
wordmark would become unreadable.

Do not recolour or redraw the mascots, remove the literacy.guide endorsement
from formal launch material, stretch the logo, place it over busy imagery, or
use the retired umbrella names in current product copy.

## Brand voice

- Speak plainly and warmly.
- Use short, action-led labels that a young reader can recognise.
- Treat the characters as helpful guides, not prizes or babyish decoration.
- Keep teacher and parent language calm, specific, and evidence-led.
- Prefer “reading adventure”, “guide”, “story”, and “practice” over invented
  terminology that needs explaining.
