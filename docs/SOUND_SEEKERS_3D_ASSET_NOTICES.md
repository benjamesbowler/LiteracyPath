# Sound Seekers 3D asset notices

## Quality standard

Sound Seekers visual work should be completed to the highest practical degree of effort and accuracy. Every asset must be checked in the running game for visual coherence, animation behavior, loading reliability, accessibility, and performance before it is described as complete.

## Shared future-game library

The broader CC0 KayKit collection and its source revisions are documented in `docs/3D_ASSET_LIBRARY.md` and indexed by `public/models/library/manifest.json`. Sound Seekers loads only the specific models used by the active scene; the remaining models are available for deliberate use in future LiteracyPath games.
