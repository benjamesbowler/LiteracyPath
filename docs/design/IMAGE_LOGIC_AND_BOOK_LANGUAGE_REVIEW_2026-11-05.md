# Book language and image logic review

The 5 November 2026 review covers all 297 runtime Guided Reading books (2,621
pages), including the 172 A–C books, and every current displayable image path.
It applies the [writing Bible](../content/STORY_BIBLE_PART_1_WRITING.md),
[canon and continuity Bible](../content/STORY_BIBLE_PART_2_CANON.md) and
[illustration standard](ILLUSTRATION_STYLE_STANDARD.md). Those documents own
future requirements; this record describes the reviewed change.

Thirty-seven page sentences in 24 books and discussion prompts in three books
were revised for ordinary, accessible language and story logic. In *Muddy Has
a Bath*, “It is time to eat” replaces “Supper starts soon.” The bath has a stable
size, clear water precedes entry, contact causes the brown water, and the clean
water follows an explicit refill. The complete ordered sequence was exercised
in the reader. Reading levels remain unchanged.

Ninety-four book illustrations and six dependent cover cards were repaired.
Other corrections include cast identity, relative size, carried props, blanket
state, a premature shadow and the six-petal stone flower. Fifty-five exact Leda
clips were generated: 37 revised page reads and 18 missing Story Quest word
clips. Decoding, signal and automated transcription checks are recorded
separately from human listening.

The current image inventory contains 11,066 paths: 10,608 directly reviewed
displayable images and 458 retained sources with verified non-display purposes.
All book pages and covers were compared in sequence. Other images were viewed
in indexed full-aspect sheets; flagged details were inspected natively. The
1,060 final repair and derivative receipts include all 765 affected shared
question cards, whose final outputs received an independent second review.
Exact hashes bind these observations to the delivered pixels.

The “yum” scene now shows breakfast at a home table with one held spoon.
Further repairs address incorrect word cues, duplicate or floating utensils,
impossible prop contact, exact animal/object counts, inconsistent characters,
pseudo-writing, generation stamps and neighboring atlas strips. Shared question
composition now accounts for one carried object, actual pot openings and front
walls, grounded height comparisons and real bridge/lever contact. Context-only
words retain audio/text access while remaining excluded from picture-only
choices.

Sixty-five obsolete images and their unused review rows were retired only after
finite current, authoring and historical-binding checks. A narrow transport
alias supplies the reviewed “look” image for old assessment paths without
rewriting immutable learner snapshots. A separate shared-media prune plan keeps
publication limits strict while retaining new or changed local files.
Three corrected legacy deliveries moved to adjacent WebPs after exact pixel,
reference and approval checks; two moves change administrative QA paths only,
and the third updates the coat fallback. The separate reviewed-retirement
validator checks fresh consumers, source graph, historical bytes and replacement
approval; the original inactive-image validator and its scope remain intact.

Evidence lives in ignored `.artifacts/book-language-continuity/` and
`.artifacts/app-image-logic-audit/`. The latter contains the complete coverage
summary, per-shard reviews, exact final repair receipts, current held-source
proof and final question-card reviews. Native generated originals remain in
their recorded source locations. Two recovered background generation receipts
explicitly distinguish recovered archived call evidence from unavailable old
raw receipt bytes.

This is an exhaustive review of the current inventory with no unresolved
confirmed findings. File/hash checks establish coverage and byte identity;
they cannot establish a subjective “0% AI slop” guarantee. Browser review does
not imply classroom observation, physical-device acceptance or human listening.
Git publication and automatic hosted delivery are recorded separately.

Verification includes a 6,778-test complete unit run, focused regressions for
subsequent format and retirement changes, 18 actual assessment-image browser
cases, reader navigation/playback controls, exact page narration, language and
visual-alignment checks. The production build retains all 1,060 reviewed image
repairs/derivatives and all 55 new Leda clips with matching hashes. Production
security preflight, public source-map exposure, ESLint, media sizes and repository
hygiene pass. Automated checks and direct agent image inspection are explicitly
distinguished from human listening or classroom observation.
