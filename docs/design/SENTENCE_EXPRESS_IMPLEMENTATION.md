# Sentence Express production upgrade

Status, 5 October 2026: the complete three-world upgrade has passed native
full outings, parent integration, all 4,710 application tests, whole lint and
the required 108 device, twelve child-surface and eighteen media browser
checks. The isolated release excludes all unfinished Word Climb changes.
The final build, scoped publication and live-byte verification remain separate
release steps; this upgrade is not live yet.

The child chooses a capital engine, couples carriage instances in order,
repairs a rusty word, fills a missing crate and picks the caboose mark. A
completed assembly waits for the deliberate **Send the train!** action. The
train then travels through the yard with sequential recorded word readback
before tally. All ninety original trains and 494 word slots remain in
`src/utils/sentenceExpressLevels.js`. The printed sentence is a model: this is
supported reconstruction and repair practice, not independent sentence
production or mastery. Repeated identical word cars remain interchangeable
physical instances with separate slots and no duplicate credit.

Easy uses Bouncy and the Meadow railway; Medium uses Chompy and Dino Canyon;
Hard uses Pip and Moonwood. Nine encoded deliveries include eighteen conductor
poses, twelve station props and three whole yard panoramas. Runtime WebPs total
3,832,902 bytes. Source PNGs, prompts, registration and the manifest retain their
actual dimensions and hashes. Registered soles and palm contacts are drawn
against measured DOM rail geometry; an unmeasured contact is explicitly absent.
The retained DOM/SVG train, original landscape and station-clock scenery remain
active recovery dependencies. Failed conductor art uses the existing physical
Pal fallback; unavailable yard/station layers reveal the retained scenery.
The registry declares `retained-illustration` and `authored-arcade` presentation.

The full-screen main container supplies the stage's width/height query context.
This keeps choices and Send within short landscape screens while retaining the
56-pixel touch floor. Coupled cars leave the choice bank without invisible empty
slots, so remaining choices stay discoverable. The engine-transfer animation
travels behind the instruction panel, preserving readable instructions while
input remains available. The rail still represents the
actual assembly; long banks and trains scroll at full readable size.

`sentenceExpressLearning.js` validates immutable first responses, supported
retries, actual explicit Send and one settled departure. Its readback owner
records only a matching current recorded clip's actual ended event. Missing,
muted, aborted and timed-out clips keep null `endedAt`; promise settlement does
not prove audio delivery. Pause aborts the current word and genuine resume
replays it. Music ducks for the current speech owner's real play event and
restores on end, failure, pause or disposal. Departure readback stops the chuff
and prevents old instruction speech from resuming over the new word.

The existing scoped `practiceSession[difficulty]` retains assembly, reversible
last-car uncoupling, actual Send/readback, journey time and canonical stage
awards. Uncoupling grants no literacy credit; recoupling retains first responses
and records supported retry. A quota error holds the exact unsaved snapshot and
blocks more choices until explicit Retry saves it. Validated legacy sidecar
aggregates are labelled legacy totals, never fabricated v2 responses, and are
removed only after durable migration. Catch-up visits have separate rehearsal
Send/departure receipts without duplicate original stamps. An already assembled
final legacy train can finish with zero new choices, but still needs its real
Send and settled departure. Zero departures is an unfinished checkpoint.

Completion uses `sentence-express-v2` and
`model-supported-printed-sentence-reconstruction-and-repair`, retaining origin,
actual response/support history and departure counts. Next and Replay begin
fresh playable seeds and preserve settled prior achievements.
