# Finished deliverable definition of done

## Decision

The user's requested outcome and exact specifications are the acceptance
contract. Work is complete only when the finished usable deliverable satisfies
every explicit in-scope requirement, passes the current applicable gates, and
has been exercised in its intended environment.

Demos, prototypes, candidates, proxies, placeholders, manifests, contact
sheets, partial packs and release-blocked assets are intermediate work unless
the user explicitly requested that form. An unmet requirement remains a blocker
or active work; it cannot be relabelled as a limitation, follow-up or completed
handoff.

This strengthens the bounded agent workflow. “Smallest scoped change” means the
smallest change that completely satisfies the brief, not a reduced endpoint.

## Required behavior

- Preserve exact qualifiers such as `all`, `every`, `exact`, `finished`,
  `production-ready`, `Moho-ready` and `ready to publish` as testable criteria.
- Identify material feasibility or authority blockers during preflight rather
  than after producing a knowingly insufficient substitute.
- Continue safe in-scope work until every acceptance criterion passes.
- Verify the actual artifact or behavior in the target tool, device, hosted
  environment, listening context or visual sequence required by the request.
- Mark a genuinely blocked task as blocked, not complete, and name the exact
  access or authority needed to resume.
- Never bypass safety, privacy, educational-validity, data-rights or release
  requirements to manufacture a completion claim.

## Concrete interpretation

`Moho-ready` requires independently usable semantic parts, true alpha, correct
registration/common canvas and direct Moho import and deformation verification.
An opaque parts sheet or release-blocked source catalogue is not equivalent.

`A background for every scene` requires an appropriate completed background
for each exact scene. A small shared master bank linked across many labels is
not equivalent unless the user explicitly requested reusable masters and each
assignment passes exact scene review.
