# Continuous QA is pass-by-exception

Human checking across the product is ongoing. Content, media, routes and devices
are treated as accepted unless the product owner or another reviewer reports a
specific defect. Missing human-review metadata must not be presented as “waiting
for approval” and must not block a release.

A reported defect is different: it is recorded, the affected item or route is
quarantined where exposure could cause harm or mis-teaching, and that quarantine
remains until the correction is verified. Automated checks protect integrity but
do not manufacture claims about a human observation.

This decision applies to media QA, Guided Reading publication, narration
listening, accessibility use and recurring teacher/child observation. It does not
remove account-access approval, school safeguarding/authority, consent/assent,
written data-processing agreements, DPIAs, or other legal and security controls
whose purpose is not content quality approval.
