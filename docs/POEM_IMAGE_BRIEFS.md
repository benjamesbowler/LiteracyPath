# Present-Deck Poem Illustrations — Art Briefs

One bespoke illustration per EL cycle poem, starring our own book characters.
Generate each at **landscape 1200×900 (4:3), WebP**, and save to the exact path
shown. The Present deck loads them automatically once they exist:

```
public/images/pals/poems/cycle-01.webp  →  cycle-27.webp
```

## Global art direction (applies to every image)
- Match our existing series art: warm, clean, bright, child-friendly cartoon, soft shading.
- **No embedded text, no letters, no speech bubbles, no captions** anywhere in the image.
- Single clear scene, generous margins, characters large and readable from across a classroom.
- Keep each character's design **identical** to the bible below (colours, clothing, species).
- The world rotates every 3 cycles: Meadow → Dino → Moonwood → repeat.

## Character bible

**Meadow Pals** (farm meadow: barn, pond, hill, log, hay bales, flowers)
- **Muddy** — friendly meadow pal who loves mud, cheerful and a bit messy.
- **Woolly** — gentle sheep-like pal, soft and calm.
- **Clucky** — busy hen-like pal, tidy and bustling.
- **Bouncy** — energetic pal who hops with playful energy.
- **Sleepy** — tired pal who often rests or naps.
- **Speedy** — fast pal, always dashing.
- **Tiny** — a very small pal (show clear scale cues).

**Dino Pals** (Sunny Hollow: Cozy Cave, Berry Bush Corner, Fernwood, Rainbow Waterfall, Long Meadow, Mount Rumble)
- **Chompy** — orange baby T-Rex, tiny arms, big round tummy, white bib, sweet hungry face.
- **Sunny** — yellow baby Triceratops, rainbow-tipped horns, bright happy look.
- **Dozy** — lavender baby Stegosaurus, sleepy half-closed eyes, small pillow.
- **Zippy** — red-and-yellow baby Velociraptor, small rainbow scarf, very speedy.
- **Honky** — coral baby Parasaurolophus, huge rainbow crest, very loud.
- **Wiggly** — pale-blue baby Diplodocus, long wavy tail, apologetic look.
- **Cheeky** — purple baby Oviraptor with orange spots, mischievous grin.

**Moonwood Tales** (enchanted forest: glowing mushrooms, Hollow Oak, Crystal Stream, Fog Marsh, Whispering Meadow)
- **Pip** — young elf, pointed ears, brown hair, green eyes, dark-green tunic, brown belt and boots.
- **Fern** — leaf fairy, leaf-green skin, fern-frond wings, amber eyes, moss-and-leaf clothes.
- **Stone** — gentle baby troll, pale grey-blue skin, enormous hands, soft eyes, simple brown clothes.
- **Glimmer** — young dragon, purple-blue scales, gold highlights, small wings, teal eyes.
- **Wren** — witch apprentice, wild dark hair, oversized dark-blue robe, big hat, green spell book.
- **Spark** — junior wizard, bright red hair, freckles, red-and-gold robe, oversized wand, floating gold sparkles.
- **Luna** — ancient owl, deep grey feathers, white face disc, amber eyes, tiny gold glasses.
- **Dewdrop** — water sprite, translucent blue-green shimmer, flowing blue-white hair.

## Briefs

### Meadow (1–3)

### Dino (4–6)
