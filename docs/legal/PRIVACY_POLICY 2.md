LiteracyPath is an early-literacy and phonics learning tool used by teachers with
their pupils (roughly ages 4–7). It is designed to be used through a school or
teacher, who authorises the limited collection of pupil information described below
(the "school consent" approach under COPPA and similar rules). We do not knowingly
collect information directly from children outside a teacher-managed context.

## Information we collect
- **Teacher account:** email address, display name, school name.
- **Pupil (entered by the teacher):** first name / display name, class, and a
  three-picture "symbol password" for typing-free login.
- **Learning activity:** progress, scores, stars, books read, assessment results.

We do **not** collect a pupil's email, home address, phone, precise location,
photos, or sensitive personal information.

## What we do not do
- No advertising.
- No selling, renting, or trading personal information.
- No third-party advertising or behavioural-tracking services.
- No marketing profiles of children.

## How we use information
Only to operate the service: sign pupils in, save and show progress to their
teacher, choose appropriate activities, and produce certificates/reports. Teacher
contact details manage the account and essential service messages.

## Storage and sharing
Processed only by infrastructure providers acting on our behalf:
- **Supabase** — database and login.
- **Vercel** — website hosting.

Shared only with these providers or where required by law. A pupil's data is visible
to their own teacher/school. The symbol password is a simple picture code for young
children, visible to the managing teacher by design — not a strong secret.

## Retention and deletion
Kept while the account is active and the school wishes to retain it. Schools,
teachers, or parents may request review, correction, or deletion at any time via
benjamesbowler@gmail.com.

## Parents' choices
Through the child's teacher or by contacting us, parents may review, restrict, or
delete their child's information. Requests may be verified with the school.

## Security
Access controls and reputable hosting providers protect the limited data we hold.

## Changes
Material changes update the date above; continued use means acceptance.
