## Operator and commercial terms

- [ ] Verify contracting legal identity, form, establishment, and address.
- [ ] Verify privacy, security, accessibility, and legal notice contacts.
- [ ] Approve authorised customer/user and child-managed-use model.
- [ ] Complete fees, term, renewal, support, SLA, taxes, and refunds.
- [ ] Approve IP, confidentiality, feedback, warranty, suspension, and termination.
- [ ] Approve liability, indemnity, insurance, governing law, venue, and disputes.
- [ ] Confirm consumer, public-sector, education, and non-waivable rights.
- [ ] Confirm precedence among order form, terms, DPA, privacy notice, and addenda.

## Data roles, purposes, and lawful basis

- [ ] Validate actual controller/processor/joint-controller roles by processing purpose.
- [ ] Approve every purpose and prohibit unauthorised secondary use.
- [ ] Validate teacher/admin, learner, operational, accessibility, and credential data categories.
- [ ] Decide treatment of accessibility/support-needs data and any special-category implications.
- [ ] Confirm no production AI receives identifiable Customer Data.
- [ ] Approve de-identification/aggregation standard or remove the proposed use.
- [ ] Verify school authority, parent/guardian consent, and learner-assent materials.

## US education and child privacy

- [ ] Apply the amended COPPA rule and current FTC school guidance.
- [ ] Validate direct notice, verified school authorisation, parent notice/consent path, review/deletion, security program, and retention policy.
- [ ] Ensure the operator does not shift COPPA responsibility to schools.
- [ ] Validate FERPA exception/direct-control/use/redisclosure/annual-notice language.
- [ ] Assess PPRA, IDEA interactions, state student-privacy laws, biometrics definitions, breach rules, and school procurement addenda.
- [ ] Define supported US states and a process for future state changes.

## UK and EU/EEA

- [ ] Validate UK/EU role and lawful-basis analysis for school and operator purposes.
- [ ] Assess Children’s code scope, best interests, age-appropriate transparency, profiling/adaptation, and high-privacy defaults.
- [ ] Review DPIA and any prior-consultation requirement.
- [ ] Complete Article 28 clauses and processing annexes.
- [ ] Determine Article 9/DPA 2018 or national education-law conditions where relevant.
- [ ] Complete UK/EU representative, DPO, establishment, supervisory-authority, and records decisions.
- [ ] Approve data-subject-rights verification, routing, exceptions, and service levels.

## Providers, regions, and transfers

- [ ] Verify Supabase and Vercel legal entities, plans, regions, replicas, backups, logs, remote access, DPAs, and onward providers.
- [ ] Remove/self-host Google Fonts or approve and disclose its request/transfer position.
- [ ] Confirm authoring-only tools are technically and operationally separated from Customer Data.
- [ ] Complete provider security/assurance review and written protection requirements.
- [ ] Approve subprocessor notice period, objection, remedy, and emergency-change process.
- [ ] Map all restricted transfers and remote access.
- [ ] Execute adequacy/SCC/UK IDTA or Addendum mechanisms and transfer assessments as required.

## Retention, rights, incidents, and recovery

- [ ] Approve active, inactivity, end-of-year, termination, security-log, error-log, access-log, and backup periods.
- [ ] Verify implemented deletion propagation, subprocessor deletion, backup expiry, and restored-backup tombstone replay.
- [ ] Exercise access, correction, export, restriction, objection, withdrawal, and deletion with a seeded learner.
- [ ] Approve incident definition, roles, 24-hour proposed Customer target, phased notice, regulator/individual analysis, and evidence handling.
- [ ] Review the completed tabletop and isolated restore evidence.
- [ ] Confirm legal hold and mandatory-retention exceptions.

## Security and accessibility

- [ ] Review independent penetration/privacy findings and remediation.
- [ ] Approve vulnerability reporting and secure evidence channel.
- [ ] Review independent WCAG 2.2 audit, disabled-user findings, VPAT/ACR decision, and accessible alternatives.

## Publication and closure

- [ ] Reconcile the final privacy policy with actual data flows and retention.
- [ ] Reconcile the public HTML page with the approved source.
- [ ] Verify plain-language and child-appropriate notices.
- [ ] Identify translated/localised notices required by customers.
- [ ] Confirm every unresolved deployment fact is closed or expressly blocks launch.
- [ ] Record approved document versions and immutable evidence.
- [ ] Set annual and change-triggered review dates.
- [ ] Update the current release issue only with genuine external approval evidence.

## Approval boundary

“Pack complete” or “EXTERNAL-READY” means the materials are organised and ready
for a qualified reviewer. It does not mean a reviewer approved them. A reviewer
must identify scope and exceptions; silence, a code commit, a passing automated
gate, or an operator’s self-review cannot close A8.10 external legal review.
