# Privacy notice source and operating record

> **Last reviewed against the product:** 22 August 2026
> **Current public version:** `2026-08-22-uk-v2`
> **Public notice:** `/privacy.html`

The HTML page above is the current public UK privacy notice. It covers the
school-authorised and anonymous try-out routes, controller/processor roles,
teacher, guardian and learner data, school-created family invitations,
released report snapshots, revocation and access audit events, Article 6 and possible Article 9 decisions, browser
storage, service/security logs, no advertising or identifiable learner AI
training, rights and complaints, current retention defaults, subprocessors and
international transfers.

Verified deployment fact: the linked Supabase production project is in Sydney,
Australia (`ap-southeast-2`). Vercel hosts the application through its global
network and its published DPA states US primary processing. These public provider
documents do not prove that the operator's actual account has an executed DPA or
completed UK transfer safeguard; those facts remain open in
`LEGAL_DEPLOYMENT_FACTS.md`.

The notice must be updated and versioned before any material change to data,
purpose, retention, user or access route, provider, region, AI use, analytics, advertising or
payment processing. The operator must retain the version accepted at teacher
account request and guardian invitation acceptance, and route child or guardian
rights requests safely through the school where the school is controller.
