# Accessibility statement operating source

> **Current public statement:** `/accessibility.html`
> **Last reviewed:** 21 August 2026

Literacy Guide aims for WCAG 2.2 Level AA and anticipatory reasonable
adjustments. The public statement records current support, known limitations,
alternative routes and the contact process. It makes no third-party certification
claim.

Accessibility checking is ongoing and pass-by-exception. A route is treated as
working unless a barrier is reported; reported barriers are recorded,
prioritised, contained where necessary, corrected and retested. Schools must
offer a suitable assisted or non-digital alternative where the digital
interaction is unsuitable for a learner.
