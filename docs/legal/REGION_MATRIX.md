# UK-first region position

> **Version:** 21 August 2026
> **Primary operating region:** United Kingdom free beta

## Current UK position

For school-directed learner processing, the school will commonly be controller
and Literacy Guide processor, but actual activity decides. The school must select
its Article 6 basis and any Article 9/Data Protection Act 2018 condition, provide
child-appropriate information, set retention, and complete a DPIA where likely
high risk. The parties need an Article 28 agreement before real learner data is
onboarded.

The linked Supabase project is in Sydney and Vercel's published position includes
US primary processing. A UK transfer chain, applicable safeguard and current ICO
data-protection test must therefore be recorded against the actual provider
accounts. Children's rights include information, access, correction, erasure,
restriction, objection and other context-dependent rights. Privacy complaints
are acknowledged within 30 days and handled without undue delay under the
post-DUAA process stated in the public notice.

The anonymous try-out stores no account or learning record and disables app
database/authentication calls, while Vercel still receives ordinary request
metadata to deliver the page. No child-to-child communication, user-generated
public content or search service is offered; the current Online Safety Act scope
assessment is therefore that no regulated user-to-user or search service has
been identified. Reassess before adding communication, uploads, public profiles
or search.

## Other regions

US, EU/EEA and other regional materials in this directory are planning and
procurement references, not current launch claims. Before intentionally offering
the data-collecting service in another region, update the public notices and
contracts, identify local child/education/privacy/consumer rules, map transfers,
and record the target deployment decision.
