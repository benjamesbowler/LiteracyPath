## Character Bible

Use the exact Meadow Pals series style.

- Art style: bright, friendly picture-book illustration; bold black outlines; flat colours; no gradients; no photorealism.
- Characters: round, chubby, big-eyed, expressive, child-friendly, and consistent across every image.
- Backgrounds: simple and uncluttered, warm vivid palette, characters large in frame.
- Shy: soft lavender-grey rabbit, colour `#C8C0D8`; large ears; big hopeful eyes; tiny pink blush marks on cheeks; usually partly hidden behind something; hesitant body posture; wants to join in but is not quite ready.
- Cuddly: orange tabby cat, colour `#E8891A`, darker orange stripes `#B56014`; round fluffy body; half-closed blissful eyes; always leaning, flopping, or seeking a gentle hug; purring lines near body; complete contentment.
- Bouncy: pale yellow lamb, colour `#FFE566`; fluffy wool; small floppy ears; coil-spring legs; often mid-hop with little yellow stars or motion marks; irrepressible glee.
- Tiny: pale brown mouse, colour `#C49A6C`; very, very small; oversized round ears; big black eyes; small pink nose; large smile; confident, cheerful, unbothered by size.
- Sunny Meadow Farm: big red barn `#CC2200` with white trim, flower meadow `#7EC850`, big oak tree with warm green leaves, rolling hills, sunny farmyard.
