# Kimi Media Request: Pip and Stone: The Loud Thing

## Story Quest Details

- Quest id: `mw_ra_c_01_pip_stone_loud_thing`
- Title: `Pip and Stone: The Loud Thing`
- Series: Moonwood Tales
- Level: C
- Folder for images: `/images/story-quests/moonwood/pip-stone-loud-thing/`
- Folder for audio: `/audio/story-quests/moonwood/pip-stone-loud-thing/`
- Image format: `.webp`
- Audio format: `.mp3`

## Character Bible

- Pip: small curious forest child, warm brown skin, round face, messy dark brown hair, moss-green tunic, little brown boots, expressive brave eyes.
- Stone: very large gentle stone-giant child, grey pebble-like skin, broad shoulders, soft kind face, moss patches on arms, slow careful posture.
- Toadling: tiny green marsh toad child, wide mouth, bright round eyes, small webbed feet, looks worried when lost and happy when found.
- Luna: wise moon moth guide, pale silver wings, calm expression, appears only as a gentle helper.

## Global Image Rules

- Simple bright storybook cartoon, clean readable scene, kindergarten-safe.
- Keep Pip, Stone, Toadling, and Luna visually consistent across all images.
- No text, letters, labels, signs, captions, speech bubbles, or readable writing inside images.
- Show clear emotion and action for the page.
- Use soft Moonwood fantasy setting: Hollow Oak, Fog Marsh, reeds, mossy stones, shallow water, gentle moonlit forest.
- Do not make the marsh scary. It should feel mysterious but safe.

## Audio Rules

- Child-friendly warm narrator voice.
- Read exactly the page text.
- Natural pacing for early readers.
- No sound effects or music.
