## Scope

Create the missing image and whole-page read-aloud audio assets for the new Moonwood Tales Level C Story Quest book `mw_ra_c_03_luna_burrow_star_shell_door`. Do not change story text, page ids, branching, or filenames.

## Global Moonwood Tales Style Guide

- Story world: warm whimsical Moonwood forest, moonlit but cozy, child-safe, painterly storybook illustration.
- Format: 16:9 horizontal WebP for each page image.
- Image framing: clear main action, readable silhouettes, not crowded, works inside the Story Quest player.
- No text, captions, labels, letters, numerals, UI, watermarks, logos, speech bubbles, or readable writing inside images.
- Keep the same visual identity as the existing Moonwood Tales assets; do not make a new series style.
- Use warm forest neutrals, moonlit blues, soft gold glow, moss greens, and gentle contrast.
- Keep character proportions, colors, clothing/accessories, and facial style consistent across every page.

## Global Audio Guide

- Format: MP3.
- Voice: warm, clear adult teacher narration, kindergarten-friendly, calm, expressive but not theatrical.
- Read exactly the page text listed below, in order.
- No extra words, title, page id, choice prompt, choice labels, sound effects, music, intro, outro, or branching commentary.
- Normalize volume consistently and leave a tiny silence at the beginning and end.

## Character Consistency Rules

- Luna: gentle Moonwood owl, soft moonlit feathers, calm curious expression, small and child-friendly.
- Burrow: small eager burrowing friend, warm brown fur, round face, expressive paws, brave but cautious.
- Pip: tiny nervous-but-brave forest friend with a small lantern when present.
- Stone: very large gentle stone-like forest friend, slow and kind, oversized hands, not scary.
- Wren: small bookish Moonwood friend with spell books; funny, careful, and expressive.
