# Kimi Image Request: Story Quest - Sam and Pam Go Out

Create 10 consistent story illustrations for a LiteracyPath decodable Story Quest.

## Story Metadata

- Title: Sam and Pam Go Out
- Skill focus: CVC and Short Vowels
- Cycle focus: short a CVC + HFW 1-25
- Target words: Sam, Pam, am, cat, mat, bag, map, van, jam
- High-frequency words: I, am, you, go, to, the, see, can, we

## Character Lock

Use the same character designs in every image:

- Sam: young boy, short brown hair, blue shirt, red shorts
- Pam: young girl, black hair in pigtails, yellow dress
- Cat: small friendly orange cat

## Global Style

- Simple bright cartoon
- Clean background
- Kindergarten-safe
- Warm, cheerful, calm
- No text, letters, labels, signs, speech bubbles, or readable writing inside images
- Consistent characters across all images
- Landscape composition, 16:9 ratio
- Leave comfortable empty space around characters so the app can crop responsively
- Export as WebP

## Page Prompts

### Page 01

Sam and Pam stand outside a small home on a sunny day, ready to go out together. Sam smiles and points down the path. Pam stands beside him holding a small bag. Bright cartoon style, clean background, no text.

### Page 02

Sam and Pam walk toward a friendly-looking van parked near the path. Sam holds a simple folded map. Pam carries the small bag. Bright cartoon style, clean background, no text.

### Page 03

Pam kneels beside a small orange cat sitting on a mat near the path. Sam stands nearby and looks happy. Bright cartoon style, clean background, no text.

### Page 04

Sam opens the small bag and Pam looks inside. A small jar of jam is visible in the bag. The scene is tidy and simple. Bright cartoon style, clean background, no text.

### Page 05

Pam holds a simple picture map while Sam and the orange cat look at it. The map should look like a picture object only, with no readable text or symbols. Bright cartoon style, clean background, no text.

### Page 06

Sam, Pam, and the orange cat sit by a mat for a short rest. The van is visible in the background. Bright cartoon style, clean background, no text.

### Page 07

Sam holds the jar of jam while Pam smiles and the cat sits beside the bag. Keep the jam jar simple with no label or text. Bright cartoon style, clean background, no text.

### Page 08

Pam points at the map while Sam points toward the van. The cat watches them from the mat. Keep the map simple with no readable writing. Bright cartoon style, clean background, no text.

### Page 09

Sam and Pam walk back toward the van with the bag and map. The orange cat follows them happily. Bright cartoon style, clean background, no text.

### Page 10

Sam, Pam, and the orange cat sit safely inside the van, smiling after their short trip. The bag and map are visible nearby. Bright cartoon style, clean background, no text.
