## Dino Pals Character Bible for Kimi

Style:
Bright, friendly picture-book illustration. Soft rounded shapes, bold outlines, warm cheerful colours. Baby dinosaurs are chubby, cute, expressive, big-eyed, and safe. No scary elements. Rain should feel playful, not frightening. 16:9 horizontal WebP images. No text inside images.

Setting:
Sunny Hollow in a rainy spring morning turning sunny. Key places: Muddy Puddle Pool, Big Flat Rock, Cozy Cave, Fernwood forest. Mount Rumble in the distance.

Sunny:
Bright yellow baby Triceratops. Three horn tips: red, orange, purple from front to back. Very big smile. Slightly bouncing with happiness. Always sees the bright side.

Grumpy:
Dark green/grey baby Ankylosaurus. Dark armour plates. Round brown club tail dragging. Permanent scowl. Noise-sensitive and rain-annoyed, secretly kind.

Dozy:
Soft lavender baby Stegosaurus. Slightly darker purple plates. Eyes always half-closed. Carries a small pillow. Sleepy, kind, warm.

Wiggly:
Pale sky-blue baby Diplodocus. Slightly darker blue spots along back and tail. Very long neck and even longer wavy tail. Apologetic expression. Tail may accidentally knock things over.

Honky:
Coral-orange baby Parasaurolophus. Huge rainbow crest like a trumpet. Loud but kind. Crest vibrates when speaking.

Continuity rule:
Use the same character model, same colours, same body shape, same markings, and same accessories on every page. Do not redesign characters between pages.
