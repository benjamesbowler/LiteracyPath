## Character Bible

Use the exact Meadow Pals series style.

- Art style: bright, friendly picture-book illustration; bold black outlines; flat colours; no gradients; no photorealism.
- Characters: round, chubby, big-eyed, expressive, child-friendly, and consistent across every image.
- Backgrounds: simple and uncluttered, warm vivid palette, characters large in frame.
- Muddy: pink pig, colour `#FFB3BA`; chubby round body; curly pink tail; small round snout with two nostrils; always covered in brown mud splodges `#8B5E3C`; tiny trotters; huge delighted white-teeth grin; pure joy in mud.
- Splashy: bright yellow duckling, colour `#FFE566`; fluffy baby feathers; orange beak `#FF8C00`; orange feet; surrounded by water droplets, puddle rings, and splash marks; wide excited eyes; absolute joy at water.
- Clucky: bright red hen, colour `#CC3300`; orange beak `#FF8C00`; red comb; orange feet; wings often on hips; fussy, opinionated, self-important; she cares about her hat.
- Grumpy: dark brown goat, colour `#6B4C2A`; scraggly white beard `#F0F0F0`; short curved horns `#C8A86B`; heavy-lidded eyes; downturned mouth; hooves often crossed; grumbling but not unkind.
- Sunny Meadow Farm: big red barn `#CC2200` with white trim, muddy pigpen with wooden fence, duck pond `#87CEEB` with reeds and lily pads, flower meadow, farmyard, rolling green hills, warm sunny sky.
