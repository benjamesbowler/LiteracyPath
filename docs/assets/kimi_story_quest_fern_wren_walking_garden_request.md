# Kimi Media Request: Fern and Wren: The Walking Garden

## Story Quest Details

- Quest id: `mw_ra_c_02_fern_wren_walking_garden`
- Title: `Fern and Wren: The Walking Garden`
- Series: Moonwood Tales
- Level: C
- Folder for images: `/images/story-quests/moonwood/fern-wren-walking-garden/`
- Folder for audio: `/audio/story-quests/moonwood/fern-wren-walking-garden/`
- Image format: `.webp`
- Audio format: `.mp3`

## Character Bible

- Fern: calm young Moonwood gardener, warm brown skin, curly dark hair, leaf-green dress or tunic, small apron, gentle focused expression.
- Wren: eager young Moonwood potion-maker, light brown skin, short auburn hair, purple cloak, small satchel of books, curious and slightly chaotic.
- Walking plants: friendly potted plants with simple root-feet, expressive leaves, safe and silly rather than scary.
- Dewdrop: small water-sprite friend near the Crystal Stream, blue-green clothes, playful smile, only appears on one page.

## Global Image Rules

- Simple bright storybook cartoon, clean readable scene, kindergarten-safe.
- Keep Fern, Wren, the plants, and Dewdrop visually consistent across all images.
- No text, letters, labels, signs, captions, speech bubbles, or readable writing inside images.
- Show the page action clearly.
- Use soft Moonwood fantasy setting: leafy garden, Hollow Oak, Crystal Stream, Burrow's tunnel, magical cauldron, gentle glowing plants.
- The walking plants should look funny and friendly, never frightening.

## Audio Rules

- Child-friendly warm narrator voice.
- Read exactly the page text.
- Natural pacing for early readers.
- No sound effects or music.
