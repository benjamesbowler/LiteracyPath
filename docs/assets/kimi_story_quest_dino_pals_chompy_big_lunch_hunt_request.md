## Dino Pals Character Bible for Kimi

Style:
Bright, friendly picture-book illustration. Soft rounded shapes, bold outlines, warm cheerful colours. Baby dinosaurs are chubby, cute, expressive, big-eyed, and safe. No scary or dark elements. Simple Level B backgrounds. 16:9 horizontal WebP images. No text inside images.

Setting:
Sunny Hollow, a warm lush valley. Key places: Cozy Cave, Berry Bush Corner, Big Flat Rock, Muddy Puddle Pool, Fernwood forest, Long Meadow. Mount Rumble sits in the distance and only puffs little heart-shaped smoke rings.

Chompy:
Orange baby T-Rex. Pale yellow tummy. Tiny arms that cannot quite reach things. Big round tummy. Small white bib. Slight drool. Sweet face. Always sniffing food. Very food-focused but kind.

Sunny:
Bright yellow baby Triceratops. Three horn tips in red, orange, purple from front to back. Enormous smile. Always cheerful and slightly bouncing with happiness.

Grumpy:
Dark green/grey baby Ankylosaurus. Dark armour plates. Round brown club tail dragging. Permanent scowl. Secretly kind.

Bouncy:
Bright lime green baby Pachycephalosaurus. Large dome head, slightly darker green. Spring-like legs. Almost always mid-air. Stars around head after bumps.

Continuity rule:
Use the same character model, same colours, same body shape, same markings, and same accessories on every page. Do not redesign characters between pages.
