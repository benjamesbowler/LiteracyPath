# Story Quest continuity references

These four sheets lock the cast, scale, recurring props and location for the
four current Moonwood Story Quests. They were resolved against connected runtime
art before the complete scene sequences were produced. Labels and multiple
views are production guidance, never page illustrations.

- `loud-thing.png`: small green-clothed Pip; smooth gray Stone in a plain brown
  tunic, about 2.7 times Pip's standing height; tiny green frog; one marsh.
- `walking-garden.png`: leaf sprite Fern; smaller Wren in her blue hat; the same
  pots, drooping leaves, bottle shapes and feet. Green helps leaves; purple grows
  feet; water removes feet. Pages must preserve those explicit rules.
- `star-shell-door.png`: natural gray owl Luna without human hands; mole Burrow
  with gold spectacles and brown vest; folded map; matching shell and door recess.
- `hidden-glow.png`: pale flowing water-nymph Dewdrop; tan-clothed elf Flint with
  brown cross-body satchel; tiny golden Glow with two round ears and curled tail;
  round bronze lantern with X braces. Glow is one fifth Flint's standing height.

These are scoped authoring references, not proof of finished scene quality.
Every page still requires direct inspection against its final text and route
state. Exact final media hashes and generation provenance live in the task's
ignored evidence; runtime cache identities and app visual reviews lock the
accepted bytes. The [production contract](../../STORY_QUEST_PRODUCTION.md) and
