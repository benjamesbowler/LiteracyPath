## Release sequence

1. Approve the target story promise and complete target manuscript for one quest.
2. Apply the manuscript to source and run wiring, level and policy gates.
3. Re-render every page marked for re-render and conduct a page-by-page image/text match.
4. Generate narration only from the locked text and conduct word-for-word plus listening-quality review.
5. Traverse every route in the browser and record route-specific consequence and ending evidence.
6. Complete the accountable editorial scorecard and update the exact source fingerprint.
7. Mark the quest approved only when the strict release gate passes with no mandatory violation.
