Five projects each contain three visits and eight different rounds per visit:
120 authored round slots and five interaction families. The picnic and brook
are available together; finishing the bridge opens the garden and parcel
clearing; the four community projects unlock the tree finale. A child can
leave an activity, choose another available project, and return to the exact
partial word. Each visit changes its place, and each project makes a lasting
repair. Five optional fireflies support free exploration.

The new interactions are sorting picture parcels into two sound baskets, and
placing a pictured object on a mat, in a basket or under a table from a spoken
instruction. The existing picture-onset, missing-letter and ordered CVC
spelling interactions return in different settings. Later missing-letter
activities include medial and final positions; repeated letters have separate
movable pieces. No activity requires dragging, a Check button or a countdown.

This is supported practice for approximately ages 5–7 who already know the
included single-letter correspondences and short vowels (the selected CVC bank
contains content introduced through cycle 12). It is not a first introduction
to all of those sounds and does not confer assessment mastery. Oral labels and
directions support vocabulary. The standalone preview has an anonymous local
save; the app has independent device-local saves for each learner, included in
privacy cleanup and teacher resets. Neither writes assessment or teacher
evidence, and neither imports the previous campaign's progress.

The 30–40-minute target is an authoring estimate: 120 rounds at roughly
12–16 seconds of child-paced listening, response and feedback, plus 6–8
minutes of actual exploration and meetings. Neither waiting nor repeated
answers are imposed to reach that length. Record actual play evidence and
duration limits separately; a fast automated run is not measured child play.
